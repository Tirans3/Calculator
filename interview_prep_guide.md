## General Interview Preparation

### Research the Company
- **Company mission, values, and culture**
- **Recent news, funding, product launches**
- **Key competitors and market position**
- **Leadership team and company structure**
- **Technologies and stack they use**

### Research the Role
- **Job description requirements**
- **Team structure and reporting**
- **Growth opportunities**
- **Day-to-day responsibilities**
- **Success metrics for the role**

### Prepare Your Story
- **Professional background summary (2-3 minutes)**
- **Key achievements and impact**
- **Career progression and decisions**
- **Why you're interested in this role/company**

## Behavioral Interview Questions

### STAR Method
**Situation** → **Task** → **Action** → **Result**

### Common Questions & Preparation
1. **"Tell me about yourself"**
   - Professional summary
   - Key achievements
   - Current focus
   - Why you're here

2. **"Why do you want to work here?"**
   - Company mission alignment
   - Growth opportunities
   - Technical challenges
   - Team/culture fit

3. **"Describe a challenging project"**
   - Technical complexity
   - Problem-solving approach
   - Team collaboration
   - Lessons learned

4. **"How do you handle conflict?"**
   - Communication skills
   - Compromise and negotiation
   - Professional relationships
   - Resolution outcomes

5. **"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"**
   - Career growth goals
   - Skill development
   - Leadership aspirations
   - Company alignment

### Leadership & Teamwork
- **Leading a project or initiative**
- **Mentoring junior developers**
- **Cross-functional collaboration**
- **Handling disagreements**
- **Building consensus**

### Problem-Solving Examples
- **Debugging complex issues**
- **Optimizing performance**
- **Handling tight deadlines**
- **Learning new technologies**
- **Overcoming technical debt**

## System Design Interview

### Common System Design Questions
1. **Design a URL shortener** (like bit.ly)
2. **Design a social media feed**
3. **Design a chat application**
4. **Design a video streaming service**
5. **Design a ride-sharing service**
6. **Design a search engine**
7. **Design a recommendation system**

### System Design Approach
1. **Clarify requirements**
   - Functional requirements
   - Non-functional requirements
   - Scale estimates

2. **High-level design**
   - Major components
   - Data flow
   - API design

3. **Detailed design**
   - Database schema
   - Algorithm details
   - Technology choices

4. **Scale the design**
   - Bottlenecks identification
   - Scaling strategies
   - Performance optimization

### Key Components to Discuss
- **Load balancers**
- **Application servers**
- **Databases** (sharding, replication)
- **Caching layers**
- **Message queues**
- **CDNs**
- **Monitoring and logging**

## Coding Interview Preparation

### Problem-Solving Strategy
1. **Understand the problem**
   - Ask clarifying questions
   - Identify constraints
   - Work through examples

2. **Plan your approach**
   - Think out loud
   - Consider edge cases
   - Discuss time/space complexity

3. **Implement the solution**
   - Write clean, readable code
   - Use meaningful variable names
   - Add comments for complex logic

4. **Test your solution**
   - Walk through test cases
   - Check edge cases
   - Verify the logic

### Common Coding Patterns
- **Array manipulation**
- **String processing**
- **Tree traversal**
- **Graph algorithms**
- **Dynamic programming**
- **Recursion and backtracking**
- **Sorting and searching**

### Time Management
- **Easy problems**: 15-20 minutes
- **Medium problems**: 25-35 minutes
- **Hard problems**: 45+ minutes

## Pre-Interview Checklist

### 24 Hours Before
- [ ] Review company research notes
- [ ] Practice elevator pitch
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask
- [ ] Check technical setup (for remote interviews)
- [ ] Plan your outfit
- [ ] Get good night's sleep

### Day of Interview
- [ ] Eat a good breakfast
- [ ] Arrive 10-15 minutes early
- [ ] Bring copies of resume
- [ ] Bring notebook and pen
- [ ] Turn off phone notifications
- [ ] Practice breathing exercises

### Technical Setup (Remote)
- [ ] Test camera and microphone
- [ ] Ensure stable internet connection
- [ ] Use headphones for better audio
- [ ] Test screen sharing
- [ ] Have backup communication method
- [ ] Clear, professional background

## During the Interview

### General Tips
- **Be authentic and enthusiastic**
- **Ask thoughtful questions**
- **Show genuine interest**
- **Demonstrate problem-solving skills**
- **Be specific with examples**
- **Admit when you don't know something**

### Technical Interview Tips
- **Think out loud**
- **Start with brute force, then optimize**
- **Write clean, readable code**
- **Test your solution**
- **Discuss trade-offs**
- **Ask for hints if stuck**

### Red Flags to Avoid
- Speaking negatively about previous employers
- Not asking any questions
- Being unprepared about the company
- Interrupting the interviewer
- Not listening to feedback
- Appearing disinterested

## Post-Interview Follow-up

### Within 24 Hours
- **Send thank-you email**
- **Reiterate interest**
- **Address any concerns raised**
- **Provide additional information if requested**

### Sample Thank-You Email
```
Subject: Thank you for the [Position] interview

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me yesterday about the [Position] role at [Company]. I enjoyed our conversation about [specific topic discussed] and learning more about [specific company/team detail].

Our discussion reinforced my enthusiasm for the opportunity to contribute to [specific project/team/goal]. I'm particularly excited about [specific aspect of the role/company].

If you need any additional information from me, please don't hesitate to reach out. I look forward to hearing about the next steps.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
